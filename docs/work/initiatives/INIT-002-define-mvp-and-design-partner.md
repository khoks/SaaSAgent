# INIT-002 — Define MVP slice and design-partner vertical

- **Status:** groomed (waiting on INIT-001)
- **Created:** 2026-04-26
- **Last updated:** 2026-04-26
- **Outcome:** A written MVP scope that is the smallest demonstrable slice of the platform, paired with an explicit design-partner vertical (real or imagined) we are building toward.

## Why
Universal platforms die when they try to be universal on day one. We need an honest MVP cut that proves the substrate is real for one vertical, then expand.

## Done when
- MVP scope document: which capabilities are in / out / deferred.
- Design-partner vertical chosen with rationale (e-commerce vs. travel vs. creative vs. other).
- Demo script: end-to-end scenario the MVP will execute.
- Anti-scope: what the MVP explicitly will NOT do.
